Cleaning

Extreme RTs

We removed 1444 trials (0.15% of the data) with RTs longer than 6 seconds, as these likely reflect participants taking breaks during the task.

illu <- illu |> filter(RT < 7)

We removed 94 trials (9.56e-03%) where RT equaled 0, as these likely reflect early responses that coincided with stimulus onset.

illu <- illu |> filter(RT > 0)

  summarize(Error = mean(Error),

  mutate(RT_SD = ifelse(is.na(RT_SD), 1, RT_SD)) |>
